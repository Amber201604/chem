## Real-world notes already captured

- No external B-field → free-electron clockwise / counterclockwise (opposite rotation senses) are equally probable; neither dominates. See `world-knowledge/electron-rotation-symmetry.md`.
- “+1” means protons outnumber electrons by 1 after electron loss — not a “positive electron.”
